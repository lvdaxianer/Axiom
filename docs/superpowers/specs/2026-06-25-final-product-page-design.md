# Final Product Page Design

**Goal:** Make the final product prototype explain the platform, orchestration approach, and technology choices at a glance.

## Scope

- Clarify what each major module does.
- Show the orchestration layer as a fixed business workflow instead of a generic drag-and-drop toy.
- Explain the selected stack and why it fits the problem.
- Make the page suitable for product reporting and stakeholder review.

## Design Summary

- The left area will act as a function map for the platform modules.
- The center canvas will show the document-review orchestration flow.
- The right panel will explain the selected node in plain language, including purpose, inputs, outputs, and evidence.
- The technical page will summarize the stack, runtime model, and hard-problem strategy.

## Expected User Outcome

- A reviewer can understand the platform in under one minute.
- A reviewer can explain how orchestration works without reading the source code.
- A reviewer can identify the product value, implementation approach, and technical trade-offs from one page.
